Who We Are

Ashwa Racing is the premier Formula Student team from RV College of Engineering, dedicated to designing, engineering, and manufacturing high-performance formula-style race cars. With over two decades of innovation, Ashwa remains one of India’s leading collegiate motorsports teams.

Engineering Focus

Our team operates across multidisciplinary domains, combining engineering rigor, operational excellence, and strategic coordination to deliver competitive race cars.

Electrical & Testing

Electrical system development, diagnostics, sensor integration, and vehicle validation.

Suspension & Administration

Kinematics design, geometry optimization, documentation, and operational coordination.

Chassis & Workshop Management

Structural frame design, fabrication supervision, and workshop resource management.

Aerodynamics

CFD-driven aero development including wings, cooling ducts, and underbody flow.

Brakes & Logistics

Brake system modeling, testing, transport planning, and competition logistics.

IT Systems

Telemetry processing, internal tools, automation, and digital infrastructure.

Sponsorship & Marketing

Industry outreach, branding, media strategy, and partner relations.

Finance

Budgeting, procurement planning, cost optimization, and financial governance.

Drivetrain & HR

Powertrain integration, performance tuning, and team development.
