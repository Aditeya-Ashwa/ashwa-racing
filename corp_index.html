Ashwa Racing is the premier Formula Student team from RV College of Engineering — dedicated to designing, engineering, and manufacturing high-performance formula-style race cars. With over two decades of competition history, Ashwa remains one of India's leading collegiate motorsports programmes.

Divisions

Nine specialised teams — combining engineering rigour, operational discipline, and strategic coordination across every aspect of the programme.

DIV · 01
Electrical & Testing
Harness design, sensor integration, DAQ, and full-vehicle validation.
DIV · 02
Suspension & Admin
Kinematics, geometry optimisation, documentation and coordination.
DIV · 03
Chassis & Workshop
Structural frame design, FEA, fabrication and resource management.
DIV · 04

DIV · 05

DIV · 06

DIV · 08
