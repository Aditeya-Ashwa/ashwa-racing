# Welcome to Ashwa Racing! We are back with a new website.

**Author:** IT  
**Date:** February 4, 2026  

---

Ashwa Racing is entering a new phase — both on the track and online.

Over the past months, the team has been working not only on engineering and testing
but also on rebuilding our digital presence from the ground up. This new website
marks a fresh start: cleaner, faster, and structured to better reflect how the team
actually operates.

![Ashwa Racing XX6C](/assets/images/blog/XX6C.jpg)

## Why a New Website?

The previous site no longer represented the scale, complexity, and direction of the
work being done within the team. As Ashwa Racing continues to grow across engineering,
manufacturing, testing, and operations, we needed a platform that could:

- Clearly communicate subsystem-level engineering efforts  
- Document progress, testing, and competition learnings  
- Better represent our partners and sponsors  
- Serve as a long-term knowledge base for future team members  

This rebuild focuses on clarity and structure rather than flash.

## What’s New?

The updated website introduces:

- Dedicated subsystem pages outlining technical scope and ownership  
- A blog to document competitions, testing phases, and engineering insights  
- Improved performance, responsiveness, and maintainability  
- A layout designed around engineering content, not marketing templates  

Most importantly, the site is designed to evolve alongside the team.

## Looking Ahead

The blog will be used to share:
- Competition reports and post-event debriefs  
- Design and testing insights across subsystems  
- Major milestones during the vehicle development cycle  
- Team updates and announcements  

This is just the beginning. As the season progresses, the site will continue to grow
with detailed documentation of the work that goes into building and running an
Ashwa Racing car.
