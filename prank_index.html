ABOUT US (OR OUR MYTHOLOGY)

Built to
Complain.
Made to
"Iterate."

Ashwa Racing is the premier* Formula Student team from RV College of Engineering — dedicated to designing, re-designing, over-designing, and then finally not manufacturing any race car at all. With over two decades of innovation (and two decades of the same freshman orientation speech), Ashwa remains one of India's most enthusiastically average collegiate motorsports teams.

We once moved a suspension pickup point 3mm and called it "a complete rearchitecture." Our design report is 247 pages. The car weighs roughly the same as the report.

* Premier in the sense that we are the only FSAE team at this specific college on this specific street.

ENGINEERING (OR WHAT WE CALL IT)

Every Car.
A New
Excuse.

Each Ashwa generation represents an "engineering progression" — which in practice means we deleted last year's entire suspension geometry at 2am, two weeks before scrutineering, and called it "a brave design philosophy pivot."

From chassis to telemetry, every system is student-built, student-broken, student-hotfix-patched with zip ties at 4am, and then competition-proven (results vary). Our ECU has been "almost configured" since October.

† RZ-XX5C did complete Endurance. We're not over it. We never will be.

SUBSYSTEMS

Departments
That Blame
Each Other

Our team operates across 9 multidisciplinary domains, each firmly believing their subsystem is the only one doing real work.

See Org Chart (It's Complicated)
01

Electrical & Testing

Wiring, rewiring, unwiring, rewiring again. Sensor integration that works great on the bench. Testing means plugging it in and hoping.

actual job: sending "is the harness done" to 4 different people at midnight
02

Suspension & Admin

Kinematics optimised 11 times. Geometry "finalised" in September, October, November, December, and February. Documentation: theoretically exists.

actual job: explaining why the geometry changed again
03

Chassis & Workshop

Structural frame design. Fabrication supervision involving four people watching one person weld. "Resource management" means hiding the last roll of aluminum tape.

actual job: finding the angle grinder that someone borrowed in October
04

Aerodynamics

CFD-driven aero that produces 73N of downforce at 80 km/h. The car has not yet reached 80 km/h. A beautiful theoretical exercise.

actual job: making renders that look incredible on instagram
05

Brakes & Logistics

Brake modelling, brake testing, brake pad procurement debate that lasted 6 weeks. Transport planning that involves one very stressed person and one minibus.

actual job: arguing about brake bias. always arguing about brake bias.
06

IT Systems

Telemetry that logs data no one has time to analyse. Internal tooling that only the person who built it can use. Digital infrastructure: three Google Drives and a shared folder named "FINAL_v2_ACTUAL_FINAL".

actual job: fixing the website at 2am after someone "just updated one line"
07

Sponsorship, Marketing & PR

Industry outreach, branding, and the art of sending LinkedIn messages to engineers at Bosch hoping they forward it to someone with a budget. Media strategy: post when the car looks shiny.

actual job: writing follow-up emails with the subject "just circling back 🙂"
08

Finance

Budgeting, procurement, and the quarterly exercise of discovering that titanium costs more than expected. Financial governance: a spreadsheet that is technically accurate as of three weeks ago.

actual job: explaining to the captain why we're ₹40,000 over on fasteners. again.
09

Drivetrain & HR

Powertrain integration, performance tuning, and "team development" which mostly means making sure no one quits before competition. Engine breaks in. Team holds together. Barely.

actual job: motivational messages in the group chat that nobody reacts to
10

Engine

Engine design, tuning, and thermal management for maximum power. The engine dyno session has been "scheduled for next weekend" since October. It is currently March.

actual job: googling "CBR600RR idle issues" at 11pm the night before dynamic events
