01 — Origin

Who We
Are

Established in 2003 under the Ashwa Mobility Foundation, Ashwa Racing is one of India's premier collegiate motorsports teams — with consistent national and global Formula Student participation spanning over two decades.

Mission & Vision
02 — Purpose

Built to
Lead.

Our vision is to become a national leader across Formula Student categories and drive sustainable mobility through student-led innovation.

Our mission is to provide industry-grade engineering exposure, foster academic-industry collaboration, and develop high-performance prototypes that push the limits of what students can build.

03 — Legacy

History &
Impact

Over two decades, Ashwa Racing has built more than 15 prototype race cars across combustion, hybrid, and electric powertrains — shaping engineers who go on to lead at India's top automotive and technology companies, and contributing to the nation's automotive R&D ecosystem.
